FAQ

What does Ramble do?

Talk through an idea or decision on your phone, wherever you are, before you forget it. Ramble turns what you say into lightly tidied text you can read on your phone or laptop.

Will it rewrite what I say?

No. Ramble removes fillers and adds punctuation. It keeps your wording rather than summarising your thoughts or turning them into something else.

Where can I use it?

Record with the iPhone app and read your words on your iPhone or through the web on your laptop.

How do I get started?

Create your account on the website, then download the iPhone app and sign in with the same account.

How much does it cost?

7-day free trial, then £9.99 a month. Cancel any time.
